# Fictional company values

These original examples are demonstration material. They do not describe a real employer.

## Collaboration
Share context, ask for feedback, and make individual contributions clear when working with others.
Example: a campaign contributor explains a handover and how feedback changed their work.

## Continuous learning
Describe an experiment, what was learned, and a concrete improvement to the next attempt.
Example: a contributor compares two content approaches and explains the next iteration.

## Accountable delivery
Agree on an achievable outcome, surface blockers early, and explain the result honestly.
Example: a contributor tracks an agreed delivery date and communicates a scope change.

Use these as observable, job-related behaviours. Do not judge personality similarity, social background, or personal beliefs.

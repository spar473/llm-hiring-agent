# Community Manager — fictional example

## Purpose
Help a small product team respond to its online community and turn recurring feedback into useful improvements.

## Responsibilities
- Maintain a shared calendar of community updates.
- Answer product questions using approved information and escalate unresolved issues.
- Summarise recurring themes with source examples for the product team.

## Required evidence
Describe a community project where you coordinated responses, explained your own contribution, and tracked the result. Academic, volunteer, personal-project, and work examples are welcome.

## Work behaviours
Share context with teammates, ask for feedback, and communicate blockers promptly.

This is a writing-style reference, not an approved role specification. Location, hours, employment terms, and eligibility must come from the manager-approved role.
